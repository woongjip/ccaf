Ways to participate include:

1. [Open call](/en/open-call) for artists' performances
2. Volunteering for those willing to join the festival
3. Stage Technology Workshop for those dreaming of working in the performing arts
4. Joining the festival staff as a professional
5. Sponsoring the festival

See [Get involved](/en/get-involved). The Korean notice board also announces calls for participants.
