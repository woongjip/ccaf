### Volunteering
Every year, the Chuncheon Arts Festival recruits volunteers to work with them. Volunteers who are interested in the performing arts join the festival to which everyone gives something, and become proud members of the festival.

### Stage Technology Workshop
Taking place during the festival, the Stage Technology Workshop is a hands-on learning program in which participants experience the entire process from preparation to the actual performance, working with professionals in stage, lighting and sound. For novices it is an opportunity to experience real stage work; for professionals, an occasion to retrain and share skills.

### Audience Club "Hangeureut"
A citizen audience club run by Ten Spoons. Members watch all festival performances together and take part in talks, movement workshops, artist talks and review sessions.
