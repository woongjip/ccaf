> If the participants of the festival maintain horizontal relationships and each of them gives something of their own, would the festival be more beautiful and enjoyable?

For Chuncheon Arts Festival, producers, stage staff and artists joined forces to make a festival that would be different from the existing ones. Before the festival, they had already worked together on the outdoor stage of Chuncheon Children's Center several times. People from different backgrounds began to collaborate in horizontal relationships to donate their talent.

Chuncheon Arts Festival started as a donation-based festival. From producers to the audience, all participants dream of becoming the main players of the festival through their participation in different forms. Such a dream is also the festival's raison d'être.

The spirit of the festival is summarized as **"십시일반" — ten spoons of rice fill a bowl** (Korean proverb). Its stage staff and system providers offer a "spoon" of the best technology and equipment. Its producers come with another "spoon" of performance planning. Promotion experts add another "spoon" of promotion and marketing. Finally, performing arts companies give us another "spoon" of performances to fill the stage prepared for them.

From 2002 (when the festival started as the Chuncheon Dance Festival) to 2011, the festival took place annually on the outdoor stage of Chuncheon Children's Center (today's KT&G Sangsang Madang Chuncheon), designed by the late architect Kim Su-geun. Later on, the festival moved to Chuncheon's major venues including the Festival Theater Mime, every August. Today the festival provides its performers and staff with fair remuneration, while keeping its conviction that ten spoons of rice fill a bowl.

In 2021, the festival expanded its finance and size in order to grow into one of Korea's representative arts festivals. In the middle of hot summer in Chuncheon, Chuncheon Arts Festival is waiting for you.

## Programmes

- **Signature** — Performing arts representing different genres and serving as indicators of contemporariness
- **Version-up** — Performances reaching perfection thanks to the festival's support for creators' existing works
- **Finder** — Emerging artists' performances or premieres
- **Arts Lab** — Support for artists from their initial phase of planning (research and workshop)
