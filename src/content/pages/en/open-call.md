| How to apply | Fill in the application form and send it to ccaf@hanmail.net with a performance video link or file attached. Write "[application]" in the e-mail subject. File name: [application]category_name of group(or artist) |

### Common support for artists
Invitation fee, space rental (including additional facilities), technical staff for the stage, house staff, promotion/management and ticketing, promotion and marketing integrated into the festival, as well as photos of invited performances.

- The invitation fee depends on the genre and size of each performance and is confirmed after discussion with the artist.
- The festival is currently unable to provide food and accommodation.
